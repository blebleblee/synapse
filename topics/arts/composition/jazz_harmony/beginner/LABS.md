# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Jazz Harmony

## Objective & Prerequisites
The objective of this hands-on lab is to introduce you to the foundational concepts of jazz harmony and to set up your environment for further exploration. By the end of this lab, you will have a basic understanding of jazz chord progressions and will have verified the setup of your musical tools. Prerequisites include a basic understanding of music theory, access to a digital audio workstation (DAW) such as Ableton Live, FL Studio, or Logic Pro, and a MIDI keyboard or virtual piano.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
Before we begin, ensure that your DAW is installed and that you have a MIDI keyboard or virtual piano connected. If you are using a virtual piano, make sure it is properly configured within your DAW.

### Step 1: Open Your DAW
- **Ableton Live:** Double-click the Ableton Live icon on your desktop or applications folder.
- **FL Studio:** Double-click the FL Studio icon on your desktop or applications folder.
- **Logic Pro:** Double-click the Logic Pro icon on your desktop or applications folder.

### Step 2: Verify MIDI Input
- **Ableton Live:** Go to `Preferences` > `MIDI`. Ensure that your MIDI keyboard is listed under `MIDI Ports` and that the `Input` is enabled.
- **FL Studio:** Go to `Settings` > `MIDI`. Ensure that your MIDI keyboard is listed under `MIDI Input` and that the `Input` is enabled.
- **Logic Pro:** Go to `Logic Pro` > `Preferences` > `MIDI`. Ensure that your MIDI keyboard is listed under `Devices` and that the `Input` is enabled.

### Step 3: Create a New Project
- **Ableton Live:** Click `File` > `New`.
- **FL Studio:** Click `File` > `New Project`.
- **Logic Pro:** Click `File` > `New` > `Blank Project`.

### Step 4: Add a MIDI Instrument
- **Ableton Live:** Click on an empty drum rack or instrument rack and select a piano sound.
- **FL Studio:** Click on an empty channel and select a piano sound from the channel settings.
- **Logic Pro:** Click on an empty software instrument track and select a piano sound from the channel strip.

### Step 5: Test MIDI Input
Play a few notes on your MIDI keyboard or virtual piano to ensure that the sound is being triggered in your DAW. You should hear the piano sound corresponding to the keys you press.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
In this exercise, we will create a simple jazz chord progression using the I-VI-II-V7 chords in the key of C major.

### Step 1: Set the Key and Time Signature
- **Ableton Live:** Click on the `Tempo` section in the bottom left corner and set the key to `C Major` and the time signature to `4/4`.
- **FL Studio:** Click on the `Piano Roll` and set the key to `C Major` and the time signature to `4/4`.
- **Logic Pro:** Click on the `Global Transport` and set the key to `C Major` and the time signature to `4/4`.

### Step 2: Enter the Chords
- **Ableton Live:** Click on the `Session View` and select the piano track. Click on the `Clip` button to create a new clip. Click on the `Note Editor` and enter the following chords:
  - Measure 1: C major 7 (C E G B)
  - Measure 2: A minor 7 (A C E G)
  - Measure 3: D minor 7 (D F A C)
  - Measure 4: G7 (G B D F#)
- **FL Studio:** Click on the `Piano Roll` and enter the following chords:
  - Measure 1: C major 7 (C E G B)
  - Measure 2: A minor 7 (A C E G)
  - Measure 3: D minor 7 (D F A C)
  - Measure 4: G7 (G B D F#)
- **Logic Pro:** Click on the `Piano Roll` and enter the following chords:
  - Measure 1: C major 7 (C E G B)
  - Measure 2: A minor 7 (A C E G)
  - Measure 3: D minor 7 (D F A C)
  - Measure 4: G7 (G B D F#)

### Step 3: Play the Chord Progression
Click the play button in your DAW to hear the chord progression. You should hear a simple jazz chord progression in the key of C major.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
Create a new chord progression using the II-V7-I chords in the key of G major. Enter the following chords:
- Measure 1: A minor 7 (A C E G)
- Measure 2: D7 (D F# A C)
- Measure 3: G major 7 (G B D F#)

Play the chord progression and verify that it sounds correct. You should hear a simple jazz chord progression in the key of G major.

By completing this lab, you have successfully set up your environment for exploring jazz harmony and have created your first working chord progression. Keep experimenting with different chord progressions and keys to deepen your understanding of jazz harmony.
