# Intermediate Operational Lab: Jazz Harmony

## Objective & Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to deepen your understanding of jazz harmony through hands-on application in a realistic production environment. You will configure a sophisticated music production setup, deploy a multi-track jazz composition, and troubleshoot common issues that arise in professional music production.

### Prerequisites
- Proficiency in a Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) such as Ableton Live, Logic Pro, or FL Studio.
- Basic knowledge of jazz theory, including chord progressions, scales, and improvisation.
- Familiarity with MIDI controllers and synthesizers.
- Understanding of audio signal processing concepts such as equalization, compression, and reverb.

## Exercise 1: Advanced Environment Configuration & Diagnostics

### Step 1: Setting Up the DAW
1. **Open your DAW** and create a new project.
2. **Configure the project settings**:
   - Set the tempo to 120 BPM.
   - Set the time signature to 4/4.
   - Ensure the project is in 24-bit, 44.1 kHz format.

### Step 2: MIDI Controller Setup
1. **Connect your MIDI controller** to your computer.
2. **Configure the MIDI settings** in your DAW:
   - Assign MIDI channels to different instruments.
   - Set up velocity curves and aftertouch sensitivity if available.

### Step 3: Instrument Setup
1. **Add a jazz drum kit**:
   - Use a built-in drum kit or import a sampled kit.
   - Configure the kit to respond to MIDI notes correctly.
2. **Add a jazz piano**:
   - Use a built-in piano or import a sampled piano.
   - Configure the piano to respond to MIDI notes correctly.
3. **Add a bass guitar**:
   - Use a built-in bass or import a sampled bass.
   - Configure the bass to respond to MIDI notes correctly.

### Step 4: Diagnostic Checks
1. **Test the MIDI controller**:
   - Play notes on the controller and ensure they trigger the correct sounds in the DAW.
   - Check for any latency issues.
2. **Test the audio output**:
   - Play back a simple chord progression and ensure the audio is clear and free of distortion.
   - Check the levels and panning of each instrument.

## Exercise 2: Building & Deploying a Production Scenario (Multi-step operational workflow)

### Step 1: Composing the Chord Progression
1. **Create a new MIDI track** for the chord progression.
2. **Enter the following chord progression**:
   - Cmaj7 - F7 - G7 - Cmaj7
   - D7 - G7 - Cmaj7 - F7
   - G7 - Cmaj7 - F7 - G7
   - Cmaj7 - F7 - G7 - Cmaj7
3. **Quantize the chords** to ensure they align perfectly with the grid.

### Step 2: Arranging the Bass Line
1. **Create a new MIDI track** for the bass line.
2. **Enter a walking bass line** that complements the chord progression:
   - C - D - E - C
   - F - G - A - F
   - G - A - B - G
   - C - D - E - C
   - D - E - F - D
   - G - A - B - G

   - C - D - E - C
   - F - G - A - F
   - G - A - B - G
   - C - D - E - C
3. **Quantize the bass line** to ensure it aligns perfectly with the grid.

### Step 3: Adding the Drums
1. **Create a new MIDI track** for the drums.
2. **Enter a simple swing groove**:
   - Use a swing feel with 8th notes.
   - Add hi-hat, snare, and bass drum patterns.
3. **Quantize the drums** to ensure they align perfectly with the grid.

### Step 4: Adding the Piano
1. **Create a new MIDI track** for the piano.
2. **Enter a simple chord voicing**:
   - Use rootless voicings for each chord.
   - Add some passing chords and extensions.
3. **Quantize the piano** to ensure it aligns perfectly with the grid.

### Step 5: Mixing and Mastering
1. **Add effects** to each track:
   - Use a reverb effect on the piano and drums.
   - Use a compressor on the bass to control dynamics.
2. **Balance the levels** of each instrument.
3. **Export the final mix** as a WAV file.

## Exercise 3: Troubleshooting Standard Failure Modes

### Issue 1: MIDI Latency
1. **Symptom**: Notes are delayed or out of sync.
2. **Solution**:
   - Check the MIDI buffer size in your DAW settings.
   - Reduce the buffer size if necessary.
   - Ensure your MIDI controller is properly connected and powered.

### Issue 2: Audio Distortion
1. **Symptom**: Clipping or distortion in the audio output.
2. **Solution**:
   - Check the levels of each instrument.
   - Reduce the gain if necessary.
   - Ensure your audio interface is properly configured.

### Issue 3: Quantization Issues
1. **Symptom**: Notes are not aligned with the grid.
2. **Solution**:
   - Use the quantize function in your DAW.
   - Adjust the quantize settings to the desired feel.
   - Manually adjust notes if necessary.

### Issue 4: Effect Chain Problems
1. **Symptom**: Effects are not processing the audio correctly.
2. **Solution**:
   - Check the order of effects in the chain.
   - Ensure each effect is properly configured.
   - Bypass effects one by one to identify the issue.

## Challenge Exercise (Real-world operational scenario)

### Scenario
You are a professional jazz musician who has been commissioned to create a new jazz composition for a live performance. The composition should feature a complex chord progression, a walking bass line, a swing drum groove, and a piano solo. The composition should be recorded in a professional studio and mastered for release.

### Task
1. **Compose a new chord progression** that is more complex than the one in Exercise 2.
2. **Arrange a walking bass line** that complements the new chord progression.
3. **Create a swing drum groove** that adds energy to the composition.
4. **Record a piano solo** that improvises over the chord progression.
5. **Mix and master the final composition**.
6. **Export the final mix** as a WAV file.

### Tips
- Use advanced chord progressions with secondary dominants and tritone substitutions.
- Experiment with different bass line techniques, such as chromatic approach notes and voice leading.
- Use a variety of drum patterns to create interest and dynamics.
- Focus on phrasing and articulation in your piano solo.
- Pay attention to the balance and clarity of each instrument in the mix.

### Submission
- Submit the final WAV file of your composition.
- Include a brief description of your compositional choices and mixing decisions.
