# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Notation

## Objective & Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to introduce you to the basics of musical notation using a digital music notation software. By the end of this lab, you will have set up your environment, created a simple musical piece, and learned how to inspect and verify your work.

### Prerequisites
- Basic understanding of music theory (notes, scales, chords).
- Access to a computer with internet connectivity.
- Installation of a music notation software (e.g., MuseScore, Finale, or Sibelius). For this lab, we will use **MuseScore**, which is free and open-source.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)

1. **Download and Install MuseScore:**
   - Visit the official MuseScore website: [MuseScore Download](https://musescore.org/en/download)
   - Choose the appropriate version for your operating system (Windows, macOS, or Linux) and download the installer.
   - Follow the installation instructions provided by the installer.

2. **Verify Installation:**
   - Open MuseScore from your applications menu or desktop shortcut.
   - You should see the MuseScore interface with a new, untitled score open.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)

1. **Create a New Score:**
   - Click on `File` in the top menu bar.
   - Select `New` to open a new score dialog.
   - Choose `Classical Score` and click `OK`. This will create a new score with a piano staff.

2. **Enter Notes:**
   - Click on the `Note Input` button (a pen icon) in the toolbar.
   - Click on the first measure of the piano staff.
   - Press the `C` key on your computer keyboard to enter a C4 note.
   - Press the `D` key to enter a D4 note.
   - Press the `E` key to enter an E4 note.
   - Press the `F` key to enter an F4 note.
   - Press the `G` key to enter a G4 note.
   - Press the `A` key to enter an A4 note.
   - Press the `B` key to enter a B4 note.
   - Press the `C` key again to enter a C5 note.

3. **Add a Chord:**
   - Click on the `Chord` button (a stacked note icon) in the toolbar.
   - Click on the second measure of the piano staff.
   - Press the `C` key, then hold `Shift` and press `E` to enter a C major chord (C-E-G).

4. **Add a Rest:**
   - Click on the `Rest` button (a rectangle icon) in the toolbar.
   - Click on the third measure of the piano staff.
   - Press the `4` key to enter a quarter rest.

5. **Save Your Score:**
   - Click on `File` in the top menu bar.
   - Select `Save As`.
   - Choose a location to save your file, name it `MyFirstScore`, and click `Save`.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)

1. **View the Score:**
   - Ensure that your score contains the notes and chord you entered in Exercise 2.
   - The first measure should have the notes C4, D4, E4, F4, G4, A4, B4, and C5.
   - The second measure should have a C major chord (C-E-G).
   - The third measure should have a quarter rest.

2. **Play the Score:**
   - Click on the `Play` button (a triangle icon) in the toolbar to hear your score.
   - You should hear the notes and chord you entered, followed by a quarter rest.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)

1. **Add a Melodic Line:**
   - Click on the `Note Input` button in the toolbar.
   - Click on the fourth measure of the piano staff.
   - Enter the following notes in sequence: E4, F4, G4, A4, B4, A4, G4, F4, E4.

2. **Save Your Updated Score:**
   - Click on `File` in the top menu bar.
   - Select `Save` to save your updated score.

3. **Play the Updated Score:**
   - Click on the `Play` button in the toolbar to hear your updated score.
   - You should hear the original notes, chord, and rest, followed by the new melodic line you added.

By completing this lab, you have successfully set up your notation environment, created a simple musical piece, and learned how to inspect and verify your work. Well done!
