## 2. Critical Data Structures, Byte-Level Encodings & Invariants

In the context of counterpoint, critical data structures can be thought of as the fundamental building blocks of a composition, such as the cantus firmus and the counterpoint voices. Byte-level encodings can be likened to the specific pitches and durations of the notes, while invariants are the rules and principles that govern the composition.

### Data Structures: Cantus Firmus and Counterpoint Voices

The cantus firmus is the primary melody around which the counterpoint voices are constructed. In terms of data structures, the cantus firmus can be represented as an array of notes, where each note is defined by its pitch and duration. The counterpoint voices can be represented as additional arrays of notes, each of which interacts with the cantus firmus according to the rules of counterpoint.

### Byte-Level Encodings: Pitches and Durations

At the byte-level, each note in a composition can be encoded as a specific value. For example, the pitch of a note can be encoded as a MIDI value, where middle C is represented by the value 60. The duration of a note can be encoded as a time value, such as a quarter note, half note, or whole note. These byte-level encodings ensure that each note is represented accurately and consistently throughout the composition.

### Invariants: Counterpoint Rules

The rules and principles of counterpoint serve as invariants that govern the composition. These invariants ensure that the composition adheres to a set of predefined criteria, resulting in a harmonically and melodically coherent piece. Some of the key invariants in counterpoint include:

- **Parallel Fifths and Octaves:** Two voices should not move in parallel fifths or octaves, as this can create a lack of independence between the voices.
- **Dissonance Resolution:** Dissonances should be properly resolved, ensuring that the composition remains stable and coherent.
- **Consonant Intervals:** Consonant intervals, such as thirds and sixths, should be used to create a harmonically pleasing composition.
