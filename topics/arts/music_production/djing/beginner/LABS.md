# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Djing

## Objective & Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to introduce beginners to the foundational tools and techniques of Djing. By the end of this lab, you will have set up a basic Djing environment, executed your first commands, and inspected the basic state of your setup.

### Prerequisites
- A computer with a modern operating system (Windows, macOS, or Linux).
- Basic familiarity with using a computer and navigating file systems.
- A Djing software application installed. For this lab, we will use **VirtualDJ**, a popular and user-friendly Djing software. You can download it from the [VirtualDJ website](https://www.virtualdj.com/).

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check

### Step 1: Install VirtualDJ
1. Visit the [VirtualDJ download page](https://www.virtualdj.com/download/index.html).
2. Download the installer for your operating system.
3. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.

### Step 2: Verify Installation
1. Open the VirtualDJ application.
2. Once the application launches, you should see the main interface with two decks (Deck 1 and Deck 2), a mixer, and a library panel on the left.

### Step 3: Load Sample Tracks
1. In the library panel, navigate to the "Samples" section.
2. Drag and drop two different sample tracks into Deck 1 and Deck 2.

### Expected Output
- You should see two different tracks loaded into Deck 1 and Deck 2, with their waveforms displayed on the decks.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment

### Step 1: Play a Track
1. Click the play button (triangle icon) on Deck 1 to start playing the track.

### Step 2: Adjust Volume
1. Use the volume fader on the mixer to adjust the volume of Deck 1. Move the fader up to increase the volume and down to decrease it.

### Step 3: Crossfade Between Tracks
1. Click the play button on Deck 2 to start playing the second track.
2. Use the crossfader in the middle of the mixer to blend between the two tracks. Push the crossfader to the left to emphasize Deck 1, to the right to emphasize Deck 2, and in the middle to mix both tracks together.

### Expected Output
- You should hear the first track playing initially, then the second track starting, and finally a smooth blend between the two tracks as you move the crossfader.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State

### Step 1: Check Track Information
1. With a track playing, hover your mouse over the waveform on the deck.
2. A tooltip should appear displaying information about the track, such as its title, artist, and BPM.

### Step 2: Monitor BPM Sync
1. Ensure both tracks are playing.
2. Observe the BPM (beats per minute) display on the mixer. VirtualDJ should automatically sync the BPM of the two tracks.

### Expected Output
- The tooltip should display accurate track information.
- The BPM display should show the BPM of the tracks and indicate that they are synchronized.

## Challenge Exercise

### Task
Create a simple mix by playing two tracks and using the crossfader to blend them together. Try to maintain a smooth transition between the tracks.

### Steps
1. Load two different tracks into Deck 1 and Deck 2.
2. Play both tracks.
3. Use the crossfader to smoothly transition from one track to the other.

### Expected Output
- A smooth and seamless transition between the two tracks, demonstrating your ability to use the crossfader effectively.

---

By completing this lab, you have successfully set up a Djing environment, executed basic commands, and inspected the state of your setup. This foundational knowledge will serve as a stepping stone for more advanced Djing techniques.
