# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Sound Design

## Objective & Prerequisites
The objective of this hands-on lab is to familiarize you with the basic setup and execution of sound design using a digital audio workstation (DAW) and a synthesizer plugin. This lab assumes you have a computer with basic internet access and the ability to download and install software. We will be using Ableton Live Lite as our DAW and the built-in Operator synthesizer for our sound design experiments.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
### Step 1: Download and Install Ableton Live Lite
1. Visit the [Ableton Live Lite download page](https://www.ableton.com/en/products/live-lite/).
2. Click on the "Download" button and follow the installation instructions for your operating system (Windows, macOS, or Linux).

### Step 2: Launch Ableton Live Lite
1. Open Ableton Live Lite from your applications folder or desktop shortcut.
2. You should see the Ableton Live Lite interface with a blank project.

### Step 3: Verify the Operator Synthesizer
1. In the Ableton Live Lite interface, click on the "Browser" tab on the left side.
2. Navigate to "Instruments" and find the "Operator" synthesizer.
3. Drag the "Operator" synthesizer into an empty MIDI track in the Session view.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
### Step 1: Create a New MIDI Clip
1. In the Session view, click on the empty cell in the first row of the track with the Operator synthesizer.
2. Right-click and select "Create Clip" or press `Shift + C`.
3. A new MIDI clip will appear in the cell.

### Step 2: Enter MIDI Notes
1. Click on the clip to enter the Clip Editor.
2. In the Clip Editor, click on the grid to add MIDI notes. Try adding a simple C major scale (C4, D4, E4, F4, G4, A4, B4, C5).
3. You should see the notes appear as vertical lines in the grid.

### Step 3: Play the MIDI Clip
1. Exit the Clip Editor by clicking outside of it.
2. Click on the clip to play it. You should hear the C major scale played by the Operator synthesizer.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
### Step 1: Observe the Waveform Display
1. In the Session view, click on the clip to play it again.
2. Observe the waveform display at the bottom of the screen. You should see the waveform of the played notes.

### Step 2: Check the Operator Synthesizer Parameters
1. In the Operator synthesizer interface, you can see the parameters such as Oscillator, Filter, and Envelope settings.
2. Adjust the parameters slightly (e.g., change the oscillator waveform or filter cutoff) and play the clip again to hear the changes.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
### Step 1: Create a Simple Melody
1. In the Clip Editor, create a simple melody using the notes you added earlier.
2. Experiment with different note durations and velocities to create a more interesting sound.

### Step 2: Share Your Work
1. Once you are satisfied with your melody, take a screenshot of your Clip Editor and Operator synthesizer settings.
2. Share your screenshot with a friend or post it in a community forum to get feedback.

By completing this hands-on lab, you should have a basic understanding of setting up Ableton Live Lite, creating MIDI clips, and experimenting with sound design using the Operator synthesizer.
