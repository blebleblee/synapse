# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Dynamics Processing
## Objective & Prerequisites
**Objective:** Understand the basics of dynamics processing in music production by setting up your environment and performing simple tasks using a Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) like Ableton Live or FL Studio.

**Prerequisites:**
- Basic knowledge of using a DAW.
- Familiarity with audio tracks and effects.
- A DAW installed on your computer (Ableton Live or FL Studio recommended).

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
**Step 1:** Open your DAW (Ableton Live or FL Studio).
- **Ableton Live:** Launch the application from your Applications folder (Mac) or Start Menu (Windows).
- **FL Studio:** Launch the application from your Applications folder (Mac) or Start Menu (Windows).

**Step 2:** Verify that your DAW is up and running.
- **Ableton Live:** Check the main interface for the Session and Arrangement views.
- **FL Studio:** Check the main interface for the Playlist, Channel Rack, and Mixer.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
**Step 1:** Load a sample audio clip.
- **Ableton Live:** Drag and drop a sample into an empty clip slot in Session view.
- **FL Studio:** Drag and drop a sample into an empty channel in the Playlist.

**Step 2:** Insert a dynamics processor.
- **Ableton Live:** Right-click on the clip and select "Add Effect" > "Dynamic" > "Compressor".
- **FL Studio:** Right-click on the channel and select "Insert" > "Dynamics" > "Compressor".

**Step 3:** Adjust the compressor settings.
- **Ableton Live:** Set the Threshold to -20 dB, Ratio to 4:1, and Attack to 10 ms.
- **FL Studio:** Set the Threshold to -20 dB, Ratio to 4:1, and Attack to 10 ms.

**Expected Output:** The audio clip should sound more compressed, with less dynamic range.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
**Step 1:** Observe the compressor's metering.
- **Ableton Live:** Look at the metering on the compressor device to see how much gain reduction is being applied.
- **FL Studio:** Look at the metering on the compressor device to see how much gain reduction is being applied.

**Expected Output:** The metering should show gain reduction when the audio clip is playing.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
**Task:** Create a short audio clip that demonstrates the effect of dynamics processing.
- Record or load a short audio clip.
- Apply a dynamics processor with different settings (e.g., a limiter with a high threshold and low ratio).
- Compare the processed clip to the original by toggling the effect on and off.

**Expected Output:** You should be able to hear the difference in dynamics between the original and processed clips.
