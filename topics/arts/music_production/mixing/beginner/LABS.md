# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Mixing
## Objective & Prerequisites
### Objective:
This lab aims to introduce beginners to the basics of mixing in a digital audio workstation (DAW). By the end of this lab, you will have set up your environment, performed your first mixing task, and inspected the basic state of your project.

### Prerequisites:
- Basic knowledge of digital audio workstations (DAW) such as Ableton Live, FL Studio, or Logic Pro.
- A DAW installed on your computer.
- Basic understanding of audio files and how to import them into a DAW.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
### Instructions:
1. **Open your DAW.**
   - Ensure that your DAW is properly installed and opens without any errors.
2. **Create a new project.**
   - In Ableton Live: `File > New > Live Set`
   - In FL Studio: `File > New > Project`
   - In Logic Pro: `File > New > Project`
3. **Import an audio file.**
   - In Ableton Live: Drag and drop an audio file into the Session view or right-click and select `Insert > Audio File`.
   - In FL Studio: Drag and drop an audio file into the Playlist or use `File > Import > Audio File`.
   - In Logic Pro: Drag and drop an audio file into the Arrange area or use `File > Import > Audio File`.

### Expected Output:
- A new project is created.
- An audio file is successfully imported into the project.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
### Instructions:
1. **Add an audio track.**
   - In Ableton Live: Right-click in the Session view and select `Create Track > Audio Track`.
   - In FL Studio: Click on the `Channels` tab and click the `+` button to add a new channel.
   - In Logic Pro: Click on the `Tracks` menu and select `New Audio Track`.
2. **Insert the imported audio file into the track.**
   - In Ableton Live: Drag the audio file from the Browser into the newly created Audio Track.
   - In FL Studio: Drag the audio file from the Playlist into the newly created channel.
   - In Logic Pro: Drag the audio file from the Arrange area into the newly created Audio Track.
3. **Adjust the volume of the track.**
   - In Ableton Live: Use the Volume fader on the Audio Track.
   - In FL Studio: Use the Volume fader on the channel.
   - In Logic Pro: Use the Volume fader on the Audio Track.
4. **Play the track.**
   - In Ableton Live: Press the `Play` button in the Transport bar.
   - In FL Studio: Press the `Play` button in the Transport bar.
   - In Logic Pro: Press the `Play` button in the Transport bar.

### Expected Output:
- An audio track is added to the project.
- The imported audio file is inserted into the track.
- The volume of the track is adjusted.
- The track plays back the audio file.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
### Instructions:
1. **Observe the waveform of the audio file.**
   - In Ableton Live: The waveform is visible in the Session view.
   - In FL Studio: The waveform is visible in the Playlist.
   - In Logic Pro: The waveform is visible in the Arrange area.
2. **Check the volume level during playback.**
   - In Ableton Live: Observe the VU meter on the Audio Track.
   - In FL Studio: Observe the VU meter on the channel.
   - In Logic Pro: Observe the VU meter on the Audio Track.

### Expected Output:
- The waveform of the audio file is visible.
- The VU meter shows the volume level during playback.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
### Instructions:
1. **Add another audio track and import a different audio file.**
2. **Adjust the volume of the new track to be slightly lower than the first track.**
3. **Play both tracks together and observe the combined output.**

### Expected Output:
- A second audio track is added with a different audio file.
- The volume of the second track is adjusted to be slightly lower than the first track.
- Both tracks play back together, and you can hear the combined output.

---

This concludes the Beginner Hands-On Lab for Mixing. You have successfully set up your environment, performed your first mixing task, and inspected the basic state of your project. Well done!
