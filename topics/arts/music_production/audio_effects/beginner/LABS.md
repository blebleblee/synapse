# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Audio Effects
## Objective & Prerequisites
**Objective:**  
In this lab, you will set up your environment for audio effects processing and execute your first commands using a basic Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) software. By the end of this lab, you will have a foundational understanding of how to apply and manipulate audio effects.

**Prerequisites:**  
- Basic knowledge of using a computer and file management.
- A DAW software installed. For this lab, we will use **Ableton Live Lite** (free version), which is user-friendly and widely used in music production. You can download it from [Ableton's website](https://www.ableton.com/en/products/live-lite/).

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
1. **Install Ableton Live Lite:**  
   - Download and install Ableton Live Lite from the official website.
   - Launch Ableton Live Lite to ensure it is installed correctly.

2. **Verify Installation:**  
   - Upon launching, you should see the Ableton Live Lite interface. This confirms that the installation was successful.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
1. **Create a New Project:**
   - Open Ableton Live Lite.
   - Click on `File` > `New` to create a new project.

2. **Add an Instrument:**
   - Click on the `Add Instrument` button in the toolbar.
   - Select any instrument from the list (e.g., `Operator`).

3. **Add an Audio Effect:**
   - Click on the `Add Effect` button in the toolbar.
   - Select `Reverb` from the list of effects.

4. **Apply the Effect:**
   - The Reverb effect will be added to the instrument channel. You can see the Reverb settings in the effect rack.
   - Adjust the `Decay` parameter to `2.00` seconds to hear the reverb effect clearly.

5. **Play a Note:**
   - Click on the `MIDI` tab in the instrument rack.
   - Click on any note in the piano roll to play it.
   - You should hear the note with a reverb effect applied.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
1. **Check Effect Parameters:**
   - In the Reverb effect rack, observe the parameters such as `Decay`, `Predelay`, and `Diffusion`.
   - Ensure that the `Decay` parameter is set to `2.00` seconds as set in Exercise 2.

2. **Listen to the Output:**
   - Play a few notes in the piano roll and listen to the output.
   - Confirm that the notes have a reverb effect applied, indicating that the effect is working correctly.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
1. **Experiment with Different Effects:**
   - Add another effect to the instrument channel (e.g., `Delay`).
   - Adjust the parameters of the Delay effect and play a note to hear the effect.
   - Try different settings and combinations of effects to see how they change the sound.

2. **Document Your Findings:**
   - Take notes on how different effects and parameter settings change the sound.
   - Share your findings with a peer or instructor to discuss your observations.

By completing this lab, you have successfully set up your environment for audio effects processing and executed your first commands in Ableton Live Lite. Keep experimenting with different effects and settings to deepen your understanding of audio effects in music production.
