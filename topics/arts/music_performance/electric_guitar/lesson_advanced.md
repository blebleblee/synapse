## 1. Theoretical Invariants & Formal Frameworks
--------------------------------------------

### 1.1. String Vibrational Modes

The electric guitar's sound is generated by the vibration of its strings. These vibrations can be described using the following mathematical framework:

*   The string's displacement `y(x,t)` can be modeled using the wave equation: `∂²y/∂t² = c² ∂²y/∂x²`
*   The string's tension `T` and mass per unit length `μ` are related to the wave speed `c` by: `c = √(T/μ)`

### 1.2. Electric Circuit Theory

The electric guitar's pickups and tone controls can be analyzed using circuit theory. The following invariants and formal frameworks are relevant:

*   The impedance `Z` of a circuit is defined as: `Z = V/I`
*   The admittance `Y` of a circuit is defined as: `Y = I/V`
*   The transfer function `H(s)` of a circuit is defined as: `H(s) = V_out(s)/V_in(s)`
