# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Electric Guitar
## Objective & Prerequisites
- **Objective:** Set up your environment for playing the electric guitar and execute basic commands.
- **Prerequisites:**
  - Familiarity with basic music theory.
  - Access to an electric guitar and a tuner.
  - A computer with a digital audio workstation (DAW) or a guitar simulation software.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check

1. **Install Guitar Pro** (or similar software) on your computer. This will be used for tablature and to simulate your guitar.
   ```bash
   # Install Guitar Pro
   brew install guitar-pro
   ```

2. **Connect Your Guitar:** Ensure your electric guitar is properly connected to your amplifier and tuner. Make sure the tuner is set to the correct pitch.

3. **Verify Tools:** Open Guitar Pro and select a clean, empty tablature. Play a clean, clear note on your guitar to ensure it's properly tuned and the software is recognizing your input.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment

1. **Open a New Tablature:** In Guitar Pro, create a new tablature. Choose a simple, clean pattern like a single note or a basic chord.

2. **Enter Tablature:** Use the software to enter the tablature for your chosen pattern. Pay attention to the correct placement of notes on the fretboard.

3. **Play Along:** Play the tablature on your guitar, ensuring it matches the software's display. Adjust your playing as needed to match the tablature.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State

1. **Check the Tuner:** Ensure your guitar is still properly tuned. If not, adjust as necessary.

2. **Review Tablature:** In Guitar Pro, review the tablature you entered. Ensure it matches the pattern you played.

## Challenge Exercise

1. **Simple Chord Progression:** In Guitar Pro, create a new tablature for a simple chord progression (e.g., A minor to C major). Enter the tablature and play it on your guitar.

2. **Experiment with Effects:** Use the software to add a basic effect (e.g., reverb) to your chord progression. Play it back and adjust the effect settings to your liking.

This lab is designed to introduce you to the basics of playing the electric guitar with a digital tool. Remember, practice is key to mastering music.
