# Operational Verification Labs: 01 Fretboard Navigation And Caged System

## Objective & Lab Setup

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to provide a comprehensive understanding of fretboard navigation and the CAGED system on the electric guitar. Through a series of practical exercises, you will develop the ability to locate notes, chords, and scales across the fretboard, enhancing your overall proficiency and fluency on the instrument.

### Lab Setup
For this lab, you will need the following:
- An electric guitar with a standard 24-fret neck.
- A tuner to ensure your guitar is in standard tuning (EADGBE).
- A guitar amplifier and speaker cabinet or headphones.
- A metronome or drum machine for rhythmic exercises.
- A guitar tablature software or app (such as TuxGuitar, Guitar Pro, or a similar tool) for visualizing chord diagrams and tablature.
- A digital audio workstation (DAW) like Ableton Live, FL Studio, or Logic Pro for recording and playback.

## Exercise 1: Foundational Practical Implementation

### Exercise 1.1: Familiarize with the CAGED System
The CAGED system is a method for visualizing the fretboard by breaking it down into five interlocking patterns based on the open chords C, A, G, E, and D. Each pattern covers a specific section of the fretboard and helps you locate notes, chords, and scales across the entire neck.

#### Steps:
1. **Open C Chord Pattern:**
   - Locate the open C chord on the 8th fret. The pattern spans from the 8th to the 12th fret.
   - Memorize the note positions within this pattern. For example, the 8th fret on the A string is C, the 9th fret on the D string is D, and so on.
   - Practice moving up and down the pattern, playing each note in sequence.

2. **Open A Chord Pattern:**
   - Locate the open A chord on the 5th fret. The pattern spans from the 5th to the 9th fret.
   - Memorize the note positions within this pattern. For example, the 5th fret on the E string is A, the 7th fret on the D string is B, and so on.
   - Practice moving up and down the pattern, playing each note in sequence.

3. **Open G Chord Pattern:**
   - Locate the open G chord on the 3rd fret. The pattern spans from the 3rd to the 7th fret.
   - Memorize the note positions within this pattern. For example, the 3rd fret on the G string is G, the 5th fret on the D string is A, and so on.
   - Practice moving up and down the pattern, playing each note in sequence.

4. **Open E Chord Pattern:**
   - Locate the open E chord on the 0th fret. The pattern spans from the 0th to the 4th fret.
   - Memorize the note positions within this pattern. For example, the 0th fret on the E string is E, the 2nd fret on the B string is D, and so on.
   - Practice moving up and down the pattern, playing each note in sequence.

5. **Open D Chord Pattern:**
   - Locate the open D chord on the 2nd fret. The pattern spans from the 2nd to the 6th fret.
   - Memorize the note positions within this pattern. For example, the 2nd fret on the D string is D, the 4th fret on the G string is A, and so on.
   - Practice moving up and down the pattern, playing each note in sequence.

### Exercise 1.2: Chord Navigation
Using the CAGED system, practice moving between different chords within each pattern.

#### Steps:
1. **C Pattern:**
   - Play the C chord at the 8th fret.
   - Move to the G chord at the 10th fret.
   - Move to the Am chord at the 12th fret.
   - Practice transitioning between these chords smoothly.

2. **A Pattern:**
   - Play the A chord at the 5th fret.
   - Move to the E chord at the 7th fret.
   - Move to the F chord at the 8th fret.
   - Practice transitioning between these chords smoothly.

3. **G Pattern:**
   - Play the G chord at the 3rd fret.
   - Move to the D chord at the 5th fret.
   - Move to the Em chord at the 7th fret.
   - Practice transitioning between these chords smoothly.

4. **E Pattern:**
   - Play the E chord at the 0th fret.
   - Move to the B chord at the 2nd fret.
   - Move to the C#m chord at the 4th fret.
   - Practice transitioning between these chords smoothly.

5. **D Pattern:**
   - Play the D chord at the 2nd fret.
   - Move to the A chord at the 4th fret.
   - Move to the Bm chord at the 6th fret.
   - Practice transitioning between these chords smoothly.

## Exercise 2: Parameter Variation & Empirical Analysis

### Exercise 2.1: Scale Navigation
Using the CAGED system, practice playing scales across the fretboard.

#### Steps:
1. **C Major Scale:**
   - Start at the 8th fret of the A string (C) and play the C major scale using the C pattern.
   - Continue the scale using the A pattern starting at the 5th fret of the E string (A).
   - Continue the scale using the G pattern starting at the 3rd fret of the G string (G).
   - Continue the scale using the E pattern starting at the 0th fret of the E string (E).
   - Continue the scale using the D pattern starting at the 2nd fret of the D string (D).

2. **A Minor Scale:**
   - Start at the 5th fret of the E string (A) and play the A minor scale using the A pattern.
   - Continue the scale using the G pattern starting at the 3rd fret of the G string (G).
   - Continue the scale using the E pattern starting at the 0th fret of the E string (E).
   - Continue the scale using the D pattern starting at the 2nd fret of the D string (D).
   - Continue the scale using the C pattern starting at the 8th fret of the A string (C).

### Exercise 2.2: Chord Progression Navigation
Using the CAGED system, practice playing chord progressions across the fretboard.

#### Steps:
1. **Cmaj7 - G7 - Am7 - F:**
   - Play the Cmaj7 chord using the C pattern at the 8th fret.
   - Move to the G7 chord using the G pattern at the 10th fret.
   - Move to the Am7 chord using the A pattern at the 12th fret.
   - Move to the F chord using the C pattern at the 11th fret.
   - Practice transitioning between these chords smoothly.

2. **Amin7 - E7 - F - C:**
   - Play the Amin7 chord using the A pattern at the 5th fret.
   - Move to the E7 chord using the E pattern at the 7th fret.
   - Move to the F chord using the C pattern at the 8th fret.
   - Move to the C chord using the C pattern at the 8th fret.
   - Practice transitioning between these chords smoothly.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection & Stress Testing

### Exercise 3.1: Fretboard Mapping Errors
Introduce errors in your fretboard mapping and analyze the resulting sound.

#### Steps:
1. **Introduce Errors:**
   - Play a C major scale using the C pattern, but intentionally play the wrong notes at the 9th and 10th frets.
   - Play a G7 chord using the G pattern, but intentionally play the wrong notes at the 5th and 6th frets.
   - Play an Am7 chord using the A pattern, but intentionally play the wrong notes at the 7th and 8th frets.

2. **Analyze Errors:**
   - Listen to the resulting sound and identify the dissonant intervals created by the incorrect notes.
   - Determine the correct notes and practice playing the scale and chord correctly.

### Exercise 3.2: Chord Progression Errors
Introduce errors in your chord progressions and analyze the resulting sound.

#### Steps:
1. **Introduce Errors:**
   - Play the chord progression Cmaj7 - G7 - Am7 - F, but intentionally play the G7 chord as a Gmaj7 at the 10th fret.
   - Play the chord progression Amin7 - E7 - F - C, but intentionally play the E7 chord as an Emaj7 at the 7th fret.

2. **Analyze Errors:**
   - Listen to the resulting sound and identify the harmonic tension created by the incorrect chords.
   - Determine the correct chords and practice playing the chord progression correctly.
