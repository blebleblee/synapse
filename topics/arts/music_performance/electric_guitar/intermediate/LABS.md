# Intermediate Operational Lab: Electric Guitar
## Objective & Prerequisites

* Objective: Configure and troubleshoot an electric guitar for a live performance, incorporating advanced techniques and tools.
* Prerequisites:
	+ Basic understanding of music theory and guitar fundamentals.
	+ Familiarity with guitar electronics and setup.
	+ Access to an electric guitar, amplifier, and effects pedals.

## Exercise 1: Advanced Environment Configuration & Diagnostics

### Task 1: Advanced Guitar Setup

* Adjust bridge and nut for optimal playing feel and intonation.
* Install a locking tremolo system for improved tuning stability.
* Configure the guitar's electronics for optimal tone and noise reduction.

### Task 2: Amplifier and Effects Pedal Configuration

* Set up a high-gain amplifier with a master volume control.
* Configure a delay pedal with tap tempo and a reverb pedal with a built-in tuner.
* Use a noise gate to minimize hum and buzz.

### Task 3: Diagnostic Tools and Techniques

* Use a multimeter to measure the guitar's output and identify any issues.
* Employ a spectrum analyzer to visualize the guitar's frequency response.
* Use a signal generator to test the guitar's electronics and effects pedals.

## Exercise 2: Building & Deploying a Production Scenario (Multi-step operational workflow)

### Task 1: Creating a Setlist

* Develop a setlist for a live performance, incorporating a mix of original songs and covers.
* Choose songs that showcase the guitar's capabilities and the performer's skills.

### Task 2: Rehearsing and Refining the Performance

* Rehearse the setlist to ensure a smooth and polished performance.
* Refine the performance by making adjustments to the guitar's setup and the effects pedals.

### Task 3: Deploying the Production Scenario

* Set up the stage and equipment for the live performance.
* Test the sound system and make any necessary adjustments.
* Perform the setlist, incorporating advanced techniques and effects.

## Exercise 3: Troubleshooting Standard Failure Modes

### Task 1: Identifying and Resolving Common Issues

* Identify common issues that can arise during a live performance, such as tuning problems or equipment failure.
* Develop strategies for resolving these issues quickly and efficiently.

### Task 2: Advanced Troubleshooting Techniques

* Use advanced diagnostic tools and techniques to identify and resolve complex issues.
* Employ problem-solving strategies to troubleshoot and resolve issues in real-time.

### Task 3: Maintaining the Equipment

* Develop a maintenance schedule for the guitar and equipment.
* Perform routine maintenance tasks to ensure optimal performance.

## Challenge Exercise (Real-world operational scenario)

### Task 1: Simulating a Live Performance

* Set up a simulated live performance environment, incorporating a stage, sound system, and audience.
* Perform a setlist, incorporating advanced techniques and effects.

### Task 2: Troubleshooting in Real-Time

* Introduce simulated issues and equipment failure during the performance.
* Troubleshoot and resolve the issues in real-time, using advanced techniques and tools.

### Task 3: Debriefing and Review

* Debrief the performance, discussing what went well and what didn't.
* Review the performance, identifying areas for improvement and developing strategies for addressing them.
