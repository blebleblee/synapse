# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Vocals
## Objective & Prerequisites
### Objective
The objective of this lab is to introduce you to the basics of vocal performance and recording. You will set up a basic recording environment, record your first vocal sample, and learn how to inspect the recording.

### Prerequisites
- A computer with a microphone and audio recording software (such as Audacity, which is free and open-source).
- Basic understanding of how to use a computer and navigate file systems.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
### Step 1: Install Audacity
1. Download Audacity from the official website: [Audacity Download](https://www.audacityteam.org/download/).
2. Follow the installation instructions for your operating system.

### Step 2: Verify Microphone Connection
1. Open Audacity.
2. Go to `Edit` > `Preferences` > `Devices`.
3. Ensure your microphone is selected as the input device.
4. Click `Test` to check if the microphone is working. You should see the waveform moving as you speak into the microphone.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
### Step 1: Record Your First Vocal Sample
1. Click the `Record` button (red circle) in the toolbar.
2. Speak or sing into the microphone for about 5-10 seconds.
3. Click the `Stop` button (yellow square) to stop recording.

### Expected Output
- You should see a waveform representing your vocal performance in the Audacity window.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
### Step 1: Play Back Your Recording
1. Click the `Play` button (green triangle) to play back your recorded sample.
2. Listen to the playback and ensure it matches what you recorded.

### Step 2: View Recording Properties
1. Select the recorded sample by clicking and dragging over the waveform.
2. Right-click the selected sample and choose `Get Info`.
3. Review the properties such as duration, sample rate, and bit depth.

### Expected Output
- You should be able to hear your recording when played back.
- The `Get Info` dialog should display the properties of your recording.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
### Task
1. Record a short phrase or a simple song.
2. Play back the recording and assess the quality.
3. Try to improve the recording by adjusting your microphone position or volume.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to record a clear and audible vocal sample.
- You should be able to identify areas for improvement in your recording technique.
