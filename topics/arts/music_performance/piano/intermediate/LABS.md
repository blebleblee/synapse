# Intermediate Operational Lab: Piano
## Objective & Prerequisites

* Objective: Configure and troubleshoot a piano for optimal performance in a live setting.
* Prerequisites:
	+ Basic understanding of piano mechanics and acoustics.
	+ Familiarity with piano tuning and maintenance.
	+ Access to a piano or digital piano emulator.

## Exercise 1: Advanced Environment Configuration & Diagnostics

### Task 1: Advanced Tuning

* Configure the piano to achieve a precise pitch standard (A440).
* Experiment with different tuning techniques (e.g., equal temperament, just intonation).
* Measure and record the piano's pitch accuracy using a digital tuner or software.

### Task 2: Environmental Optimization

* Adjust the piano's placement and orientation to minimize sound reflections and optimize resonance.
* Experiment with different types of piano covers or silencers to reduce noise pollution.
* Measure and record the piano's sound quality using acoustic analysis software.

## Exercise 2: Building & Deploying a Production Scenario (Multi-step operational workflow)

### Task 1: Piano Preparation

* Clean and polish the piano to ensure optimal performance.
* Check and replace any worn or damaged parts (e.g., strings, hammers).
* Perform a thorough tuning and voicing to achieve optimal sound quality.

### Task 2: Stage Setup

* Position the piano in the performance space to maximize visibility and sound quality.
* Configure the piano's pedals and other features for optimal performance.
* Test the piano's sound system and ensure proper connectivity.

### Task 3: Performance Deployment

* Develop a set list or performance plan for the piano.
* Rehearse and refine the performance to ensure technical and artistic excellence.
* Deploy the performance in a live setting, incorporating any necessary technical adjustments.

## Exercise 3: Troubleshooting Standard Failure Modes

### Task 1: Common Issues

* Identify and troubleshoot common issues (e.g., pitch drift, sound quality degradation).
* Develop strategies for preventing and resolving these issues.

### Task 2: Advanced Troubleshooting

* Develop a systematic approach to troubleshooting complex issues (e.g., mechanical failures, electronic malfunctions).
* Practice applying this approach to real-world scenarios.

## Challenge Exercise (Real-world operational scenario)

### Task: Live Performance Deployment

* Deploy the piano in a live performance setting, incorporating all skills and knowledge acquired in this lab.
* Troubleshoot any issues that arise during the performance, applying the skills and strategies developed in this lab.
* Reflect on the experience and identify areas for improvement.
