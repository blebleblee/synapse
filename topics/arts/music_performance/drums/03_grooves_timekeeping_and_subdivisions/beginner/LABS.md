# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Drums 03 Grooves Timekeeping And Subdivisions
## Objective & Prerequisites
### Objective
In this lab, you will learn the basics of timekeeping and subdivisions on the drums. You will set up your practice environment, execute simple exercises, and inspect your progress to ensure you are on the right track.

### Prerequisites
- A drum kit or practice pad.
- A metronome (physical or digital).
- Basic understanding of drumming techniques covered in previous lessons.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check
### Setting Up Your Practice Space
1. **Arrange Your Drums:** Ensure your drum kit is set up in a comfortable position. If using a practice pad, place it on a stable surface.
2. **Set Up Your Metronome:** If using a digital metronome, make sure it is charged and ready to use. If using a physical metronome, ensure it is wound and functioning properly.

### Sanity Check
1. **Test Your Metronome:** Set the metronome to a comfortable tempo (e.g., 60 BPM) and listen to the click to ensure it is clear and consistent.
2. **Warm-Up:** Spend a few minutes warming up your hands and arms with simple strokes on your drum kit or practice pad.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment
### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Start with a Simple Beat:** Begin by playing a basic beat using the hi-hat, snare, and bass drum. A common beginner beat is:
   - Hi-hat on every count (1-2-3-4)
   - Snare on counts 2 and 4
   - Bass drum on count 1
2. **Play Along with the Metronome:** Set your metronome to 60 BPM and start playing the beat. Focus on keeping time with the clicks.
3. **Subdivide the Beat:** Once you feel comfortable with the basic beat, try subdividing the hi-hat clicks. Instead of playing the hi-hat on every count, play it on every eighth note (1-and-2-and-3-and-4-and).

### Expected Outputs
- You should be able to maintain a steady rhythm with the metronome.
- You should be able to play the basic beat accurately.
- You should be able to subdivide the hi-hat clicks smoothly.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State
### Reading Your Progress
1. **Listen to Your Playing:** Record yourself playing the exercises and listen back to ensure you are keeping time accurately.
2. **Adjust as Needed:** If you find yourself rushing or dragging, adjust the tempo of the metronome or focus on your timing.

## Challenge Exercise
### Simple Self-Test
1. **Try a Different Tempo:** Set your metronome to a different tempo (e.g., 80 BPM) and play the same beat and subdivisions.
2. **Add a Fill:** After playing four bars of the basic beat, try adding a simple fill on the fifth bar. For example, you could play a series of eighth notes on the snare and hi-hat.

By completing this lab, you should have a better understanding of timekeeping and subdivisions on the drums. Keep practicing to build your skills and confidence.
