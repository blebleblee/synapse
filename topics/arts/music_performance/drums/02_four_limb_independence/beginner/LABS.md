# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Drums 02 Four Limb Independence

## Objective & Prerequisites
The objective of this lab is to introduce you to the concept of four-limb independence on the drums. This skill is crucial for developing a strong foundation in drumming, allowing you to play complex rhythms and patterns. 

**Prerequisites:**
- Basic understanding of drumming and familiarity with the drum set.
- A drum kit with at least a snare, hi-hat, bass drum, and one tom.
- A metronome or drum machine (optional but recommended).

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check
Before we begin, let's ensure that your drum set is properly set up and that you have the necessary tools.

**Steps:**
1. **Check Your Drum Set:**
   - Ensure that all drums are securely tightened and properly tuned.
   - Make sure that your cymbals are mounted correctly and not loose.
   - Check that your drumsticks are in good condition and not broken.

2. **Set Up Your Metronome:**
   - If you are using a metronome, set it to a comfortable tempo (e.g., 60 BPM).
   - Familiarize yourself with how to start and stop the metronome.

**Expected Outcome:**
- Your drum set is ready to play.
- You can start and stop the metronome as needed.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment
In this exercise, we will start by practicing simple patterns that focus on using different limbs independently.

**Steps:**
1. **Start with the Bass Drum:**
   - Play the bass drum on every beat of the metronome.
   - Focus on maintaining a steady rhythm.

2. **Add the Snare Drum:**
   - Play the snare drum on beats 2 and 4 of the metronome.
   - Continue playing the bass drum on every beat.

3. **Incorporate the Hi-Hat:**
   - Play the hi-hat on every beat of the metronome.
   - Keep playing the bass drum on every beat and the snare drum on beats 2 and 4.

4. **Add the Tom:**
   - Play the tom on beat 3 of the metronome.
   - Maintain the bass drum, snare, and hi-hat patterns.

**Expected Outcome:**
- You should be able to play a simple pattern using all four limbs independently.
- The pattern should sound like this: BD-Sn-HH-Tom-HH-Sn-HH-BD (where BD = Bass Drum, Sn = Snare, HH = Hi-Hat, Tom = Tom).

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State
In this exercise, we will focus on listening to the sounds produced by each limb and ensuring that they are clear and distinct.

**Steps:**
1. **Listen to Each Limb:**
   - Play each limb individually and listen to the sound it produces.
   - Ensure that each sound is clear and not muffled.

2. **Listen to the Combined Pattern:**
   - Play the full pattern from Exercise 2.
   - Listen to how each limb contributes to the overall sound.
   - Make sure that each limb is heard clearly and that the pattern is consistent.

**Expected Outcome:**
- You should be able to clearly hear each limb in the pattern.
- The pattern should sound balanced and consistent.

## Challenge Exercise
Create a simple pattern using four-limb independence that is different from the one in Exercise 2. Try to use different combinations of limbs and beats.

**Steps:**
1. **Choose a New Pattern:**
   - Decide on a new pattern using all four limbs.
   - Make sure that each limb is used independently.

2. **Practice the New Pattern:**
   - Start with a slow tempo and gradually increase the speed.
   - Focus on maintaining a steady rhythm and clear sounds from each limb.

3. **Record Your Practice:**
   - Use a smartphone or recording device to record your practice.
   - Listen back to the recording to evaluate your performance.

**Expected Outcome:**
- You should be able to create and play a new pattern using four-limb independence.
- The pattern should sound clear and consistent.
