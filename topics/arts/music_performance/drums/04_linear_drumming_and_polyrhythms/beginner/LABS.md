# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Drums 04 Linear Drumming And Polyrhythms

## Objective & Prerequisites
The objective of this lab is to introduce you to the basics of linear drumming and polyrhythms. By the end of this lab, you should be able to understand and execute simple linear patterns and begin to explore polyrhythms. 

**Prerequisites:**
- Basic understanding of drumming and rhythm.
- Access to a drum kit or electronic drum pads.
- A metronome or drumming software (optional but recommended).

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
For this lab, you do not need to install any software, but having a metronome will be helpful. If you are using a drumming software, ensure it is installed and running.

**Steps:**
1. **Set up your drum kit or electronic drum pads.**
2. **Connect your metronome or ensure your drumming software is ready to use.**
3. **Adjust the metronome to a comfortable tempo (e.g., 60 BPM).**

**Sanity Check:**
- **Play a simple beat (e.g., bass drum on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4) to ensure your setup is correct.**
- **Listen to the metronome and try to match your playing with its beat.**

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
In this exercise, you will practice a basic linear drumming pattern and then introduce a simple polyrhythm.

**Steps:**
1. **Start with a simple 4/4 beat:**
   - Bass drum on 1 and 3.
   - Snare drum on 2 and 4.
   - Play this pattern for 1 minute, focusing on even timing and volume.

2. **Add a hi-hat pattern:**
   - Play the hi-hat on every beat (1, 2, 3, 4).
   - Combine this with the previous pattern and play for 1 minute.

3. **Introduce a simple polyrhythm:**
   - Play a bass drum pattern of 3 hits per measure (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
   - Keep the snare and hi-hat patterns as before.
   - Play this pattern for 1 minute, focusing on keeping the snare and hi-hat steady while the bass drum pattern changes.

**Expected Outputs:**
- **Simple 4/4 beat:** A steady, even rhythm with bass drum and snare on alternating beats.
- **With hi-hat:** A consistent pattern with the hi-hat adding a steady flow to the beat.
- **Polyrhythm:** A challenging but rewarding pattern where the bass drum plays a different rhythm against the steady snare and hi-hat.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
In this exercise, you will reflect on your performance and make adjustments.

**Steps:**
1. **Record yourself playing each pattern.**
2. **Listen back to the recordings and identify areas for improvement.**
3. **Adjust your timing, volume, and technique as needed.**

**Expected Outputs:**
- **Self-reflection:** A clear understanding of your current skill level and areas for improvement.
- **Improved performance:** A noticeable improvement in your ability to play the patterns accurately and smoothly.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
Create a new pattern by combining a different polyrhythm with the snare and hi-hat patterns.

**Steps:**
1. **Choose a new bass drum pattern (e.g., 4 hits per measure: 1, 2, 3, 4).**
2. **Combine this with the snare and hi-hat patterns.**
3. **Play the new pattern for 1 minute.**

**Expected Outputs:**
- **New pattern:** A unique rhythm that challenges your timing and coordination.
- **Confidence:** Increased confidence in your ability to create and execute complex patterns.
