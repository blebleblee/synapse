# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Strings
## Objective & Prerequisites
### Objective
This lab is designed to introduce beginners to the world of string instruments, focusing on setting up a basic environment and executing your first commands. By the end of this lab, you should be able to set up your string instrument, perform a basic exercise, and understand the initial state of your setup.

### Prerequisites
- A string instrument (violin, guitar, cello, etc.)
- A bow if using a violin, cello, or viola
- Basic understanding of how to hold and position your instrument
- Access to a quiet space where you can practice without disturbance

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
### Setting Up Your Instrument
1. **Unpack your instrument** carefully, ensuring all parts are present and in good condition.
2. **Tune your instrument** using a tuner or a pitch pipe. Each string should produce a clear, resonant sound at the correct pitch.
3. **Adjust your bow** (if applicable) by tightening or loosening the bow hair until it produces a clear, even tone when drawn across the strings.

### Sanity Check
- **Play each string** individually to ensure they are all in tune and produce a clear sound.
- **Check the bow** (if applicable) by drawing it across each string and listening for an even, clear tone.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
### Playing a Simple Scale
1. **Position your instrument** correctly and hold it securely.
2. **Place your fingers** on the appropriate frets or fingerboard positions for the scale you are learning (e.g., C major scale on a guitar).
3. **Play each note** of the scale, starting from the lowest and moving to the highest.
4. **Repeat the scale** several times, focusing on producing a clear, even tone for each note.

### Expected Outputs
- You should be able to play each note of the scale clearly and in tune.
- The transition between notes should be smooth and even.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
### Checking Your Progress
1. **Record yourself** playing the scale using a smartphone or other recording device.
2. **Listen back** to the recording to assess your performance.
3. **Identify areas** for improvement, such as tuning, tone, or technique.

### Simple Outputs
- A recording of you playing the scale.
- Self-assessment of your performance, noting any areas that need improvement.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
### Playing a Simple Song
1. **Choose a simple song** that uses only the notes of the scale you learned (e.g., "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star").
2. **Practice playing the song** several times, focusing on maintaining a steady rhythm and clear tone.
3. **Record yourself** playing the song and listen back to assess your performance.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to play the song clearly and in tune.
- The rhythm should be steady, and the transitions between notes should be smooth.
