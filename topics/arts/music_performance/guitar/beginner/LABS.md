# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Guitar
## Objective & Prerequisites
### Objective
This lab is designed to introduce beginners to the basics of playing the guitar. By the end of this lab, you should be able to set up your guitar, tune it, and play your first chords.

### Prerequisites
- A guitar (acoustic or electric)
- A tuner (either a physical tuner or a smartphone app)
- Basic understanding of the guitar's parts (neck, body, strings, etc.)

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check
### Step 1: Unpack Your Guitar
- Carefully unpack your guitar and inspect all parts to ensure nothing is damaged.
- Ensure you have all the necessary accessories (strings, picks, strap if applicable).

### Step 2: Tune Your Guitar
- Use your tuner to tune each string to the correct pitch. The standard tuning for a guitar is EADGBE (from the 6th string to the 1st string).
- Start with the 6th string (low E) and work your way up to the 1st string (high E).
- Pluck each string and adjust the tuning pegs until the tuner indicates that the string is in tune.

### Expected Outcome
- All six strings should be in tune and produce clear, resonant sounds.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment
### Step 1: Learn the Basic Chord Shapes
- **C Major Chord:**
  - Place your ring finger on the 3rd fret of the 5th string.
  - Place your middle finger on the 2nd fret of the 4th string.
  - Place your index finger on the 1st fret of the 2nd string.
  - Strum all six strings.

- **G Major Chord:**
  - Place your middle finger on the 3rd fret of the 6th string.
  - Place your ring finger on the 2nd fret of the 5th string.
  - Place your index finger on the 3rd fret of the 1st string.
  - Strum all six strings.

- **Em Major Chord:**
  - Place your ring finger on the 2nd fret of the 4th string.
  - Place your middle finger on the 2nd fret of the 3rd string.
  - Place your index finger on the 2nd fret of the 2nd string.
  - Strum all six strings.

### Step 2: Practice Switching Between Chords
- Start with the C Major chord, then switch to the G Major chord, and finally to the Em Major chord.
- Practice switching between these chords smoothly and with minimal muting of strings.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to play and switch between the C, G, and Em chords without significant buzzing or muting.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State
### Step 1: Listen to Your Playing
- Play each chord individually and listen carefully to the sound.
- Ensure that each chord is clear and resonant.

### Step 2: Record Yourself
- Use a smartphone or a recording device to record yourself playing the chords.
- Listen back to the recording to assess your technique and sound quality.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to hear each chord clearly and without any noticeable issues.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
### Challenge
- Try to play a simple chord progression: C - G - Em - G.
- Play each chord for four beats, and then move to the next chord.
- Repeat the progression several times.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to play the chord progression smoothly and with confidence.

Congratulations on completing your first hands-on lab with the guitar! Keep practicing and exploring the world of music.
