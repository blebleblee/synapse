# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Bass Guitar
## Objective & Prerequisites
### Objective
This lab is designed to introduce beginners to the bass guitar. By the end of this lab, you will have set up your bass guitar, tuned it, and played your first notes.

### Prerequisites
- A bass guitar (electric or acoustic)
- An amplifier (if using an electric bass)
- A tuner (either a digital tuner or a smartphone app)
- Basic understanding of musical notes and scales

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check
### Step 1: Assemble Your Bass Guitar
1. Ensure all parts of your bass guitar are properly assembled.
2. Attach the strings to the tuning pegs and the bridge.
3. Plug in your bass guitar to the amplifier if you are using an electric bass.

### Step 2: Tune Your Bass Guitar
1. Use your tuner to check the pitch of each string.
2. Turn the tuning pegs to adjust the pitch until each string is in tune.
3. The standard tuning for a bass guitar is E-A-D-G (from the thickest to the thinnest string).

### Expected Outcome
- Your bass guitar should be properly assembled and tuned to the correct pitch.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment
### Step 1: Familiarize Yourself with the Fretboard
1. Identify the strings and their corresponding notes (E-A-D-G).
2. Locate the frets on the neck of the bass guitar.

### Step 2: Play Your First Note
1. Place your index finger on the E string at the first fret.
2. Pluck the string with your right hand.
3. Repeat this process on the A, D, and G strings, each time placing your finger on the first fret.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to produce a clear sound from each string when you pluck it.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State
### Step 1: Listen to Your Notes
1. Play each note you learned in Exercise 2.
2. Listen carefully to the sound produced by each note.

### Step 2: Adjust Your Playing Technique
1. If the notes sound muted or unclear, adjust your finger placement and plucking technique.
2. Practice placing your finger firmly on the fret and plucking the string with a clear, crisp motion.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to produce clear, well-defined notes from each string.

## Challenge Exercise: Simple Self-Test for Beginners
### Step 1: Play a Simple Scale
1. Play the E minor pentatonic scale (E-G-A-B-D) starting from the open E string.
2. Move up the neck of the bass guitar, placing your fingers on the appropriate frets for each note.

### Step 2: Practice and Repeat
1. Practice playing the scale several times.
2. Focus on producing a clear, even sound for each note.

### Expected Outcome
- You should be able to play the E minor pentatonic scale smoothly and confidently.

Congratulations! You have completed your first hands-on lab with the bass guitar. Keep practicing to improve your skills and explore new techniques.
