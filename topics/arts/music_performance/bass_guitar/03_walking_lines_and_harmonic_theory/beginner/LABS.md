# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Bass Guitar 03 Walking Lines And Harmonic Theory
## Objective & Prerequisites
**Objective:** Understand and practice the basics of walking bass lines and harmonic theory on the bass guitar. This lab will guide you through setting up your practice environment, creating your first walking bass line, and analyzing its harmonic structure.

**Prerequisites:**
- A bass guitar and an amplifier or a bass practice amp.
- Basic knowledge of bass guitar playing and chord theory.
- Access to a metronome or a drum machine.
- A music notation software or a simple notebook to write down your exercises.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check (Exact commands to verify tools)
**Setting Up Your Practice Environment:**
1. **Tune Your Bass Guitar:** Ensure your bass guitar is properly tuned to standard tuning (E-A-D-G).
2. **Set Up Your Amplifier:** Connect your bass guitar to your amplifier and adjust the volume to a comfortable level.
3. **Metronome:** Use a metronome to keep a steady beat. Set it to a moderate tempo (e.g., 120 BPM).

**Sanity Check:**
- Play each string of your bass guitar to ensure they are in tune.
- Play a simple C major chord and a G major chord to check your setup and sound quality.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment (Step-by-step guided instructions with expected outputs)
**Creating Your First Walking Bass Line:**
1. **Choose a Chord Progression:** Start with a simple chord progression, such as C major - G major - Am7 - F major.
2. **Determine the Root Notes:** Identify the root notes of each chord in the progression (C, G, A, F).
3. **Create a Walking Line:** Connect the root notes with a smooth, stepwise bass line. Here’s an example:
   - C major: C - D - E - C
   - G major: G - A - B - G
   - Am7: A - B - C - A
   - F major: F - G - A - F

**Expected Outputs:**
- You should be able to play a smooth, stepwise bass line that connects the root notes of the chords.
- The line should flow naturally and fit the harmonic structure of the progression.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State (Reading logs or simple outputs)
**Analyzing Your Walking Bass Line:**
1. **Listen to the Line:** Play your walking bass line along with the chord progression and listen to how it sounds.
2. **Evaluate the Harmony:** Check if the bass line complements the chords and adds depth to the harmony.
3. **Make Adjustments:** If necessary, adjust the notes in your walking bass line to better fit the harmonic structure.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple self-test for beginners)
**Create a New Walking Bass Line:**
- Choose a different chord progression, such as D minor - G major - A7 - D minor.
- Create a walking bass line for this new progression.
- Play the line along with the chord progression and evaluate its effectiveness.

**Expected Outcome:**
- You should be able to create and play a walking bass line for a new chord progression.
- The line should demonstrate an understanding of harmonic theory and fit well with the chords.
