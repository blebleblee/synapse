# Beginner Hands-On Lab: Bass Guitar 02 Rhythm Muting And Grooves

## Objective & Prerequisites
**Objective:** Understand and practice rhythm muting techniques and basic grooves on the bass guitar. This lab will guide you through setting up your practice environment, executing basic muting techniques, and creating simple grooves.

**Prerequisites:**
- A bass guitar with at least 4 strings.
- An amplifier or a practice amp.
- A cable to connect your bass to the amp.
- A metronome (digital or analog).
- Basic knowledge of bass guitar tuning and playing simple chords or scales.

## Exercise 1: Environment Setup & Sanity Check
Before we begin, let's ensure everything is set up correctly.

1. **Tune Your Bass Guitar:** Use a tuner to ensure your bass is in tune. Standard tuning for a bass guitar is E-A-D-G (low to high).
2. **Connect Your Bass to the Amp:** Plug your bass guitar into the amplifier using the appropriate cable.
3. **Set Up Your Metronome:** Set your metronome to a comfortable tempo, typically around 60 BPM for beginners.

**Sanity Check:**
- Play an open E string and ensure it matches the pitch on your tuner.
- Turn up the volume on your amp to a comfortable level.
- Start your metronome and listen to the click to ensure it's functioning.

## Exercise 2: Your First Working Experiment
In this exercise, we will practice muting techniques and create a simple groove.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. **Muting Technique:**
   - Place your right-hand thumb on the strings near the bridge of the bass.
   - Play a simple note (e.g., the E string) and immediately press your thumb down on the strings to mute the sound.
   - Practice muting after each note to create a percussive effect.

2. **Simple Groove:**
   - Set your metronome to 60 BPM.
   - Play the following pattern on the E string:
     - Play the E string on the first beat.
     - Mute the string on the second beat.
     - Play the E string on the third beat.
     - Mute the string on the fourth beat.
   - Repeat this pattern for 1 minute.

**Expected Output:**
- You should hear a steady rhythm with a clear note followed by a muted click on each beat.

## Exercise 3: Inspecting Basic State
In this exercise, we will evaluate your progress and ensure you are executing the muting technique correctly.

### Instructions:
1. **Record Your Practice:**
   - Use a smartphone or a recording device to record yourself playing the simple groove.
2. **Listen Back:**
   - Play back the recording and listen for the clarity of the notes and the effectiveness of the muting.
   - Ensure that the muting is crisp and that the notes are clear and in time with the metronome.

## Challenge Exercise (Simple Self-Test for Beginners)
Create a variation of the simple groove using different strings and notes.

### Instructions:
1. **Choose a New String:**
   - Select a different string (e.g., the A string).
2. **Create a New Pattern:**
   - Play a note on the first beat.
   - Mute the string on the second beat.
   - Play a different note on the third beat.
   - Mute the string on the fourth beat.
3. **Repeat for 1 Minute:**
   - Practice this new pattern and record yourself playing it.

**Expected Outcome:**
- You should be able to create a new groove using different strings and notes, maintaining the rhythm and muting technique learned in the lab.
