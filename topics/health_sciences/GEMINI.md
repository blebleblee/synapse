## Core Technical & Operational Invariants

### 1. Strength, Hypertrophy & Exercise Biomechanics
- Resistance training mechanics: bar path kinematics for squat/bench/deadlift, joint moment arms, shear vs. compressive spinal loads, and active muscle length-tension curves.
- Periodization and loading: progressive overload protocols, RPE (Rating of Perceived Exertion) and RIR (Reps in Reserve) calibration, weekly volume landmarks (MEV, MAV, MRV), and deload week fatigue dissipation.
- Mobility and injury prevention: active vs. passive range of motion, reciprocal inhibition stretching, joint capsule mobilization, and eccentric tendon remodeling.

### 2. Emergency First Aid, Trauma Care & Wilderness Survival
- Tactical trauma management: the MARCH protocol:
  - **M (Massive Bleeding):** CAT tourniquet application (high and tight, windlass rotation, windlass clip lock), hemostatic gauze wound packing, and pressure dressings.
  - **A (Airway):** Head-tilt chin-lift, recovery position, nasopharyngeal airway (NPA) insertion.
  - **R (Respiration):** Vented chest seals for sucking chest wounds, needle decompression landmarks.
  - **C (Circulation):** Radial pulse assessment, shock management, pelvic binder application.
  - **H (Hypothermia / Head):** Active warming blankets, insulation from cold ground, GCS neurological check.
- Wilderness navigation & survival: 1:24,000 topographic map reading, magnetic declination adjustment, compass resection triangulation, water filtration (0.1 micron hollow fiber + chemical halogenation), and hypothermia wraps.

### 3. Culinary Science, Fermentation & Baking
- Bread making and sourdough: Baker's percentages, hydration calculation, autolyse phase gluten development, bulk fermentation temperature curves, and Dutch oven steam retention for crust gelatinization.
- Culinary thermodynamics: Maillard reaction thresholds (140°C–165°C), collagen-to-gelatin conversion at prolonged low temperatures (71°C–82°C), sous-vide temperature precision, and water activity ($a_w$) safety limits for curing and smoking.
- Emulsion and sauce mechanics: permanent vs. temporary emulsions, lecithin surfactant mechanics, reduction density, and starch retrogradation prevention.
