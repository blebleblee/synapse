# Hands-On Operational Lab: Great Ball Contraption
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to build, configure, and deploy a Great Ball Contraption (GBC) using LEGO parts. You will learn about mechanical systems, diagnostics, and troubleshooting through hands-on exercises. By the end of this lab, you will be able to construct a functional GBC, diagnose issues, and troubleshoot common failures.

### Lab Prerequisites
- LEGO Technic parts (specific sets recommended: 42055, 42056, 42057)
- LEGO Digital Designer or similar software for design and planning
- A flat, stable surface to build on
- Basic understanding of LEGO Technic building techniques

## Exercise 1: Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

### Step 1: Design and Planning
1. **Design the GBC:**
   - Use LEGO Digital Designer to design your GBC. Ensure you have a clear plan for the structure, including the number of modules and their connections.
   - Save your design and export a parts list.

2. **Parts List:**
   - Review the parts list and ensure you have all necessary components.
   - Organize your LEGO pieces according to the parts list.

### Step 2: Building the GBC
1. **Build the Modules:**
   - Start by building individual modules of the GBC. Focus on precision and alignment to ensure smooth operation.
   - Use the parts list as a guide and refer to the LEGO Digital Designer model for reference.

2. **Connect the Modules:**
   - Connect the modules together, ensuring that the gears and axles align properly.
   - Test each connection to ensure smooth rotation and no binding.

### Step 3: System State Inspection
1. **Inspect the GBC:**
   - Visually inspect the GBC for any misalignments or loose parts.
   - Check that all gears and axles are properly engaged and rotating smoothly.

2. **Test the GBC:**
   - Place a ball at the start of the GBC and observe its journey through the system.
   - Note any issues or areas where the ball gets stuck or the system fails.

### Expected Command Outputs and State Inspection Steps
- **Visual Inspection:** Ensure all parts are correctly assembled and no loose pieces are present.
- **System Test:** The ball should travel smoothly through the GBC without getting stuck or causing the system to fail.

## Exercise 2: Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

### Step 1: Building the Production Scenario
1. **Build the Full GBC:**
   - Using the design and parts list from Exercise 1, build the full GBC.
   - Ensure all modules are connected and the system is stable.

2. **Configure the GBC:**
   - Adjust the height and angle of the modules to ensure smooth ball flow.
   - Test the GBC with multiple balls to ensure it can handle a continuous flow.

### Step 2: Deploying the GBC
1. **Deploy the GBC:**
   - Place the GBC on a flat, stable surface.
   - Ensure there is enough space for the balls to travel through the system.

2. **Test the GBC:**
   - Place multiple balls at the start of the GBC and observe their journey through the system.
   - Ensure the system operates smoothly and efficiently.

### Expected Command Outputs and State Inspection Steps
- **Visual Inspection:** Ensure all parts are correctly assembled and no loose pieces are present.
- **System Test:** The balls should travel smoothly through the GBC without getting stuck or causing the system to fail.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Debugging

### Step 1: Fault Injection
1. **Introduce a Fault:**
   - Introduce a fault into the GBC by misaligning a gear or loosening a connection.
   - Observe the effect of the fault on the system.

2. **Identify the Fault:**
   - Inspect the GBC to identify the source of the fault.
   - Determine the cause of the fault and how it affects the system.

### Step 2: Failure Analysis
1. **Analyze the Failure:**
   - Analyze the failure to determine the root cause.
   - Consider the impact of the fault on the system and the potential consequences.

2. **Document the Failure:**
   - Document the failure and the steps taken to identify and analyze it.
   - Include any relevant images or diagrams to illustrate the failure.

### Step 3: Debugging
1. **Fix the Fault:**
   - Correct the fault by realigning the gear or tightening the connection.
   - Test the GBC to ensure the fault has been resolved.

2. **Verify the Fix:**
   - Place multiple balls at the start of the GBC and observe their journey through the system.
   - Ensure the system operates smoothly and efficiently.

### Expected Command Outputs and State Inspection Steps
- **Fault Injection:** Introduce a fault and observe the effect on the system.
- **Failure Analysis:** Identify and document the root cause of the fault.
- **Debugging:** Correct the fault and verify the fix by testing the GBC.

## Challenge Lab: Independent Advanced Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Advanced Design
1. **Design an Advanced GBC:**
   - Design an advanced GBC with additional features, such as a ball sorter or a ball elevator.
   - Use LEGO Digital Designer to create a detailed design and export a parts list.

2. **Build the Advanced GBC:**
   - Build the advanced GBC using the design and parts list.
   - Ensure all modules are connected and the system is stable.

3. **Test the Advanced GBC:**
   - Place multiple balls at the start of the GBC and observe their journey through the system.
   - Ensure the system operates smoothly and efficiently.

### Scenario 2: Troubleshooting
1. **Introduce Multiple Faults:**
   - Introduce multiple faults into the GBC by misaligning gears, loosening connections, or blocking the ball path.
   - Observe the effect of the faults on the system.

2. **Identify and Fix the Faults:**
   - Inspect the GBC to identify the source of the faults.
   - Determine the cause of the faults and how they affect the system.
   - Correct the faults by realigning gears, tightening connections, or clearing the ball path.
   - Test the GBC to ensure the faults have been resolved.

### Expected Command Outputs and State Inspection Steps
- **Advanced Design:** Design and build an advanced GBC with additional features.
- **Troubleshooting:** Introduce multiple faults, identify and fix them, and verify the fix by testing the GBC.
