# Hands-On Operational Lab: Gear Ratios
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to understand and apply gear ratios in LEGO builds. You will explore how different gear ratios affect speed and torque, build a simple LEGO mechanism, and troubleshoot common issues related to gear ratios.

### Prerequisites
- A set of LEGO gears and axles.
- A LEGO baseplate.
- A LEGO motor (if available).
- A computer with access to a LEGO Digital Designer or similar software for visualization.
- Basic understanding of LEGO building techniques.

## Exercise 1: Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

### Step 1: Understanding Gear Ratios
Gear ratios describe the relationship between the speed and torque of two gears meshed together. The gear ratio is calculated as the number of teeth on the driven gear divided by the number of teeth on the driving gear.

### Step 2: Building a Simple Gear Train
1. **Build a Gear Train:**
   - Start with a large gear (40 teeth) as the driving gear.
   - Attach a medium gear (24 teeth) to the large gear.
   - Attach a small gear (16 teeth) to the medium gear.
   - Ensure all gears are properly meshed.

2. **Calculate Gear Ratios:**
   - Large to Medium Gear Ratio: \( \frac{40}{24} = 1.67 \)
   - Medium to Small Gear Ratio: \( \frac{24}{16} = 1.5 \)
   - Overall Gear Ratio: \( 1.67 \times 1.5 = 2.5 \)

### Step 3: System State Inspection
- **Inspect the Gear Train:**
  - Ensure all gears are properly aligned and meshed.
  - Check for any wobbling or misalignment.
  - Observe the speed and torque of the gears as you manually turn the driving gear.

## Exercise 2: Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

### Step 1: Design a LEGO Mechanism
- **Design a Mechanism:**
  - Use LEGO Digital Designer to design a simple mechanism that uses the gear train.
  - Ensure the mechanism includes a motor if available.

### Step 2: Build the Mechanism
- **Build the Mechanism:**
  - Follow the design from LEGO Digital Designer.
  - Attach the motor to the driving gear if available.
  - Ensure all parts are securely attached.

### Step 3: Deploy and Test
- **Deploy and Test:**
  - Power on the motor if available.
  - Observe the speed and torque of the mechanism.
  - Adjust the gear ratios if necessary to achieve the desired performance.

### Expected Command Outputs and State Inspection
- **Expected Output:**
  - The mechanism should move smoothly with the expected speed and torque based on the gear ratios.
- **State Inspection:**
  - Inspect the mechanism for any wobbling or misalignment.
  - Ensure all gears are properly meshed and aligned.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Debugging

### Step 1: Introduce a Fault
- **Introduce a Fault:**
  - Introduce a fault by misaligning one of the gears.
  - Observe the effect on the mechanism.

### Step 2: Diagnose the Fault
- **Diagnose the Fault:**
  - Inspect the mechanism for misalignment.
  - Check the gear ratios to ensure they are correct.

### Step 3: Debug and Fix
- **Debug and Fix:**
  - Realign the misaligned gear.
  - Test the mechanism again to ensure it is functioning correctly.

### Expected Command Outputs and State Inspection
- **Expected Output:**
  - The mechanism should move smoothly without any wobbling or misalignment.
- **State Inspection:**
  - Inspect the mechanism for proper alignment and meshing of gears.
