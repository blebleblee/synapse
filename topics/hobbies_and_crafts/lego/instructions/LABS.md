# Hands-On Operational Lab: Instructions
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to provide a comprehensive guide to building, configuring, and deploying LEGO models. We will explore the mechanics of LEGO construction, perform diagnostics, and troubleshoot common issues. This lab will also challenge you to apply your knowledge in advanced scenarios.

### Prerequisites
- A set of LEGO bricks (preferably a complete set for a specific model, such as a LEGO City or Space model).
- A flat, stable workspace.
- LEGO instructions for the model you are building.
- Optional: A digital camera or smartphone for taking photos of your progress.

## Exercise 1: Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

### Step 1: Familiarize Yourself with the Instructions
- **Action:** Open the LEGO instructions for your model.
- **State Inspection:** Review the table of contents, parts list, and any special notes or tips provided.

### Step 2: Perform a Parts Count
- **Action:** Count the number of each type of LEGO piece in your set.
- **State Inspection:** Compare your count with the parts list in the instructions to ensure you have all the necessary pieces.

### Expected Output
- A verified parts list with no discrepancies.

## Exercise 2: Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

### Step 1: Build the Base
- **Action:** Follow the instructions to build the base of your model.
- **State Inspection:** Ensure that all pieces are securely connected and that the base is stable.

### Step 2: Add Structural Components
- **Action:** Continue building according to the instructions, focusing on structural components like walls and supports.
- **State Inspection:** Check that all connections are tight and that the structure is stable.

### Step 3: Add Detailing and Decorations
- **Action:** Complete the model by adding detailing and decorations.
- **State Inspection:** Verify that all pieces are in place and that the model looks as intended.

### Expected Output
- A fully assembled LEGO model that matches the instructions.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Debugging

### Fault Injection Scenario
- **Action:** Intentionally leave out a critical piece during the assembly process.
- **State Inspection:** Attempt to complete the model and observe where the issue arises.

### Debugging Workflow
- **Action:** Identify the missing piece and locate it in your parts list.
- **State Inspection:** Reassemble the model with the missing piece and verify that the issue is resolved.

### Expected Output
- A fully assembled LEGO model with no missing pieces.

## Challenge Lab: Independent Advanced Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Modify the Model
- **Action:** Modify your LEGO model by adding a new feature or changing an existing one.
- **State Inspection:** Document your changes and take photos of the modified model.

### Scenario 2: Build a Custom Model
- **Action:** Design and build a custom LEGO model using your own creativity.
- **State Inspection:** Document the design process, provide a parts list, and take photos of the final model.

### Expected Output
- A modified or custom LEGO model with detailed documentation.

---

This lab guide provides a structured approach to building LEGO models, from initial exploration to advanced customization. By following these steps, you will gain a deeper understanding of LEGO construction techniques and problem-solving skills.
