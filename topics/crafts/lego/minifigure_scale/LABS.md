# Hands-On Operational Lab: Minifigure Scale
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to guide you through the process of creating, deploying, and debugging minifigure scale models using LEGO bricks. You will progress through four levels of complexity, starting from basic exploration to advanced fault injection and debugging.

### Lab Prerequisites
- A set of LEGO bricks (preferably a variety of sizes and colors).
- A flat, stable workspace.
- Basic understanding of LEGO building techniques.
- Optional: LEGO Digital Designer or a similar digital modeling software.

## Lab Level 1 (Beginner): Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

### Task: Build a Simple Minifigure
1. **Gather Parts:**
   - 1x Head
   - 1x Torso
   - 2x Arms
   - 2x Legs
   - 1x Pair of Hands
   - 1x Pair of Feet

2. **Assembly Steps:**
   - Attach the head to the torso.
   - Attach the arms to the torso.
   - Attach the legs to the torso.
   - Attach the hands to the arms.
   - Attach the feet to the legs.

3. **State Inspection:**
   - Inspect the minifigure for stability.
   - Ensure all parts are securely connected.

### Expected Outcome
A stable, fully assembled minifigure with all parts correctly attached.

## Lab Level 2 (Intermediate): Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

### Task: Build a Minifigure with Accessories
1. **Gather Parts:**
   - 1x Head
   - 1x Torso
   - 2x Arms
   - 2x Legs
   - 1x Pair of Hands
   - 1x Pair of Feet
   - 1x Hat
   - 1x Tool (e.g., hammer, wrench)
   - 1x Vehicle or other accessory

2. **Assembly Steps:**
   - Follow the steps from Level 1 to assemble the basic minifigure.
   - Attach the hat to the head.
   - Attach the tool to one of the hands.
   - Attach the vehicle or accessory to the torso or legs as appropriate.

3. **State Inspection:**
   - Inspect the minifigure for stability.
   - Ensure all parts are securely connected.
   - Test the functionality of the accessory (e.g., can the minifigure hold the tool, can the vehicle move).

### Expected Outcome
A stable, fully assembled minifigure with accessories that function as intended.

## Lab Level 3 (Advanced): Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Root-Cause Debugging

### Task: Introduce a Fault and Debug
1. **Fault Injection:**
   - Introduce a fault by intentionally misaligning a part (e.g., attaching the arm at an incorrect angle).

2. **Failure Analysis:**
   - Observe the minifigure's behavior (e.g., instability, difficulty moving).
   - Identify the faulty part.

3. **Root-Cause Debugging:**
   - Inspect the faulty part for misalignment or improper connection.
   - Correct the misalignment or improper connection.

4. **State Inspection:**
   - Inspect the minifigure for stability.
   - Ensure all parts are securely connected.

### Expected Outcome
A stable, fully assembled minifigure with all parts correctly aligned and connected.

## Lab Level 4 (Master Challenge): Independent Advanced Scenarios

### Task: Create a Complex Scene
1. **Gather Parts:**
   - A variety of LEGO bricks and minifigures.
   - Accessories (e.g., vehicles, buildings, trees).

2. **Design a Scene:**
   - Use LEGO Digital Designer or a similar digital modeling software to design a complex scene.
   - Consider including multiple minifigures, vehicles, and buildings.

3. **Assembly Steps:**
   - Follow the design to assemble the scene.
   - Ensure all parts are securely connected.

4. **State Inspection:**
   - Inspect the scene for stability.
   - Ensure all parts are securely connected.
   - Test the functionality of the scene (e.g., can the vehicles move, can the minifigures interact with the environment).

### Expected Outcome
A stable, fully assembled complex scene with multiple minifigures, vehicles, and buildings that function as intended.
