# Hands-On Operational Lab: Microscale
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

### Objective
This lab guide will walk you through the process of creating, configuring, and deploying microscale LEGO models. You will learn how to explore, diagnose, and inspect system states, as well as how to handle and debug failures. By the end of this lab, you will be able to independently create advanced LEGO microscale models.

### Prerequisites
- A set of LEGO bricks, preferably microscale pieces.
- A flat, stable workspace.
- Basic understanding of LEGO building techniques.
- Optional: LEGO digital design software (e.g., LEGO Digital Designer, LDraw).

## Exercise 1: Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

### Task 1: Exploring Microscale LEGO Pieces
- **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with the different types of microscale LEGO pieces.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Lay out a variety of microscale LEGO pieces on your workspace.
  2. Identify and categorize the pieces by their function (e.g., beams, plates, connectors).
  3. Take note of the dimensions and unique features of each piece.

### Task 2: Diagnostics and Inspection
- **Objective:** Learn how to inspect and diagnose issues with your LEGO pieces.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Inspect each piece for any signs of damage or wear.
  2. Test the connection between pieces to ensure they fit snugly and securely.
  3. Document any issues you find and plan how to address them.

### Expected Output
- A well-organized collection of microscale LEGO pieces.
- A list of any issues found during inspection.

## Exercise 2: Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

### Task 1: Designing a Microscale Model
- **Objective:** Design a simple microscale LEGO model.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Choose a simple object to model (e.g., a car, a building).
  2. Sketch your design on paper or use LEGO digital design software.
  3. Create a parts list based on your design.

### Task 2: Building the Model
- **Objective:** Build the microscale LEGO model according to your design.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Follow your design and parts list to build the model.
  2. Pay attention to the alignment and stability of each piece.
  3. Take photos of the building process for reference.

### Task 3: Configuring and Deploying
- **Objective:** Configure and deploy your microscale LEGO model.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Place the model in a suitable location for display.
  2. Take photos of the final product.
  3. Document any adjustments made during deployment.

### Expected Output
- A completed microscale LEGO model.
- Photos of the building and deployment process.
- A list of adjustments made during deployment.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Debugging

### Task 1: Introducing a Fault
- **Objective:** Introduce a fault into your microscale LEGO model.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Deliberately misalign or damage a piece in your model.
  2. Document the fault and its impact on the model.

### Task 2: Diagnosing the Fault
- **Objective:** Diagnose the fault in your microscale LEGO model.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Inspect the model for signs of the fault.
  2. Identify the piece or connection that is causing the issue.
  3. Document your findings.

### Task 3: Debugging the Fault
- **Objective:** Debug the fault in your microscale LEGO model.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Replace or repair the faulty piece.
  2. Realign or reinforce the connection if necessary.
  3. Test the model to ensure the fault is resolved.

### Expected Output
- A documented fault and its impact on the model.
- A corrected microscale LEGO model.
- Photos of the fault and the corrected model.

## Challenge Lab: Independent Advanced Scenarios

### Task 1: Design and Build a Complex Microscale Model
- **Objective:** Design and build a complex microscale LEGO model.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Choose a more complex object to model (e.g., a detailed vehicle, a miniature city).
  2. Design the model using LEGO digital design software.
  3. Create a detailed parts list and build the model.
  4. Deploy the model and document the process.

### Task 2: Fault Injection and Debugging
- **Objective:** Introduce and debug faults in your complex microscale LEGO model.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Introduce multiple faults into your model.
  2. Diagnose and document each fault.
  3. Debug each fault and test the model to ensure it is functioning correctly.

### Expected Output
- A completed complex microscale LEGO model.
- Photos of the building and deployment process.
- Documentation of faults, diagnosis, and debugging.

### AI Mentor Guidance
- **Objective:** Receive guidance from an AI mentor.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Use AI tools to assist with design and troubleshooting.
  2. Consult with an AI mentor for feedback and suggestions.
  3. Document the AI mentor's guidance and its impact on your project.

### Expected Output
- A list of AI mentor suggestions and their implementation.
- Photos and documentation of the final project with AI mentor guidance.

---

This lab guide provides a comprehensive approach to working with microscale LEGO models, from design to deployment, and includes exercises for fault injection and debugging. By following these steps, you will gain valuable skills in LEGO building and problem-solving.
