# Hands-On Operational Lab: Architecture
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to guide you through the process of building, configuring, and deploying a production scenario using LEGO bricks. You will learn how to explore, diagnose, and inspect system states, as well as how to handle and debug faults. This lab is designed to enhance your skills in LEGO architecture and problem-solving.

### Prerequisites
- A set of LEGO bricks (preferably a large set with a variety of pieces).
- A flat, stable surface to build on.
- A ruler or measuring tape.
- A digital camera or smartphone for taking photos of your builds.
- Basic understanding of LEGO building techniques.

## Exercise 1: Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

### Task 1: Exploring LEGO Pieces
- **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with the different types of LEGO pieces available.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Sort the LEGO pieces by type (e.g., bricks, plates, tiles, beams).
  2. Identify the dimensions and properties of each type.
  3. Take photos of each type of piece.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of sorted LEGO pieces with annotations of their dimensions and properties.

### Task 2: Building a Simple Structure
- **Objective:** Build a simple structure using LEGO bricks.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Choose a simple design, such as a small tower or a bridge.
  2. Follow the instructions or build it freely.
  3. Take photos of the structure from different angles.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the simple structure with annotations of its components and dimensions.

### Task 3: Diagnostics and System State Inspection
- **Objective:** Inspect the structure for stability and alignment.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Apply gentle pressure to the structure to check for wobbling.
  2. Measure the dimensions of the structure using a ruler or measuring tape.
  3. Take photos of the structure under pressure.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the structure under pressure with annotations of any wobbling or misalignment.

## Exercise 2: Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

### Task 1: Designing a Complex Structure
- **Objective:** Design a complex structure using LEGO bricks.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Choose a complex design, such as a castle or a spaceship.
  2. Sketch the design on paper or use a digital tool.
  3. Create a parts manifest listing all required pieces.
- **Expected Output:** A sketch of the complex structure and a parts manifest.

### Task 2: Building the Complex Structure
- **Objective:** Build the complex structure using LEGO bricks.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Follow the instructions or build it freely.
  2. Take photos of the structure at different stages of construction.
  3. Ensure all pieces are correctly placed and aligned.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the complex structure at different stages of construction with annotations of any issues.

### Task 3: Configuring and Deploying the Structure
- **Objective:** Configure and deploy the structure in a real-world setting.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Choose a location for the structure.
  2. Assemble the structure in the chosen location.
  3. Take photos of the final structure in its deployed location.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the final structure in its deployed location with annotations of any issues.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Debugging

### Task 1: Injecting Faults
- **Objective:** Introduce faults into the structure to simulate real-world issues.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Remove a few pieces from the structure.
  2. Introduce misalignments or wobbling.
  3. Take photos of the structure with faults.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the structure with faults with annotations of the issues.

### Task 2: Analyzing Failures
- **Objective:** Analyze the faults to determine the root cause.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Identify the faulty pieces or misalignments.
  2. Measure the dimensions and properties of the faulty pieces.
  3. Take photos of the faulty pieces and misalignments.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the faulty pieces and misalignments with annotations of the root causes.

### Task 3: Debugging and Fixing Faults
- **Objective:** Debug and fix the faults in the structure.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Replace the faulty pieces or realign the misalignments.
  2. Apply gentle pressure to the structure to check for stability.
  3. Take photos of the fixed structure.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the fixed structure with annotations of any remaining issues.

## Challenge Lab: Independent Advanced Scenarios

### Task 1: Design and Build a Unique Structure
- **Objective:** Design and build a unique structure using LEGO bricks.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Choose a unique design, such as a futuristic city or a mythical creature.
  2. Sketch the design on paper or use a digital tool.
  3. Create a parts manifest listing all required pieces.
  4. Build the structure using LEGO bricks.
  5. Take photos of the structure at different stages of construction.
  6. Ensure all pieces are correctly placed and aligned.
  7. Deploy the structure in a real-world setting.
  8. Take photos of the final structure in its deployed location.
- **Expected Output:** A sketch of the unique structure, a parts manifest, photos of the structure at different stages of construction, and photos of the final structure in its deployed location with annotations of any issues.

### Task 2: Fault Injection and Debugging
- **Objective:** Introduce faults into the unique structure and debug them.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Remove a few pieces from the structure.
  2. Introduce misalignments or wobbling.
  3. Take photos of the structure with faults.
  4. Identify the faulty pieces or misalignments.
  5. Measure the dimensions and properties of the faulty pieces.
  6. Take photos of the faulty pieces and misalignments.
  7. Replace the faulty pieces or realign the misalignments.
  8. Apply gentle pressure to the structure to check for stability.
  9. Take photos of the fixed structure.
- **Expected Output:** Photos of the structure with faults, photos of the faulty pieces and misalignments, and photos of the fixed structure with annotations of any remaining issues.

### Task 3: Documentation and Presentation
- **Objective:** Document and present your unique structure and debugging process.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Create a detailed report of your design, construction, fault injection, and debugging process.
  2. Include photos, sketches, and annotations in the report.
  3. Present your report to an AI mentor or a group of peers.
- **Expected Output:** A detailed report of your design, construction, fault injection, and debugging process with photos, sketches, and annotations.

---

This lab guide provides a comprehensive approach to building, configuring, and deploying LEGO structures, as well as handling and debugging faults. By following the exercises and challenge lab, you will enhance your skills in LEGO architecture and problem-solving.
