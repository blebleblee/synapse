# Hands-On Operational Lab: Illegal Builds
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

**Objective:**  
In this lab, you will explore the creative and sometimes unconventional ways to build structures and mechanisms using LEGO. We will delve into techniques that may not follow traditional LEGO building guidelines, but will challenge your creativity and problem-solving skills. This lab is designed to push the boundaries of what you can achieve with LEGO, focusing on structural integrity, precision, and innovative design.

**Prerequisites:**
- A set of LEGO bricks (preferably a mix of different sizes and types).
- A flat, stable surface to work on.
- A ruler or measuring tape.
- A camera or smartphone to document your builds.
- Basic understanding of LEGO building techniques.

## Exercise 1: Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

**Objective:**  
Explore unconventional LEGO building techniques and document your findings. Perform diagnostics on your builds to ensure structural integrity and precision.

**Steps:**
1. **Choose a Theme:** Decide on a theme for your illegal build. This could be anything from a futuristic city to a mechanical contraption.
2. **Design Your Build:** Sketch your design on paper or digitally. Focus on unconventional elements such as unsupported structures, non-standard connections, or unique mechanisms.
3. **Build Your Structure:** Start building your structure using the LEGO bricks. Pay attention to how the pieces fit together and how the structure stands.
4. **Document Your Build:** Take photos or videos of your build process and the final structure. Label key components and note any challenges you faced.
5. **Diagnostics:** Inspect your build for structural integrity. Check for wobbly parts, unsupported sections, or any signs of instability. Use a ruler or measuring tape to ensure precision in your measurements.

**Expected Output:**
- Photos or videos of your build process and final structure.
- A list of key components and challenges faced.
- Inspection notes on structural integrity and precision.

**State Inspection Steps:**
- Visually inspect the structure for any wobbly parts or unsupported sections.
- Measure critical dimensions using a ruler or measuring tape to ensure precision.

## Exercise 2: Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

**Objective:**  
Build a more complex illegal structure that incorporates multiple mechanisms and unconventional elements. Configure and deploy your structure to test its functionality.

**Steps:**
1. **Design Your Structure:** Create a detailed design for your structure, including multiple mechanisms and unconventional elements. Use a digital tool or software to create a blueprint.
2. **Build Your Structure:** Follow your blueprint to build your structure. Pay close attention to the connections between different parts and the overall stability of the structure.
3. **Configure Mechanisms:** Configure any mechanisms in your structure, such as gears, pulleys, or levers. Test each mechanism individually to ensure it works as intended.
4. **Deploy Your Structure:** Place your structure in a suitable location and test its functionality. Observe how the structure behaves under different conditions and make any necessary adjustments.
5. **Document Your Build:** Take photos or videos of your build process and final structure. Label key components and note any challenges you faced.

**Expected Output:**
- Photos or videos of your build process and final structure.
- A list of key components and challenges faced.
- Inspection notes on structural integrity and precision.
- Test results for each mechanism.

**State Inspection Steps:**
- Visually inspect the structure for any wobbly parts or unsupported sections.
- Measure critical dimensions using a ruler or measuring tape to ensure precision.
- Test each mechanism individually to ensure it works as intended.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Debugging

**Objective:**  
Introduce faults into your illegal build and analyze the failures. Debug the issues to improve the structural integrity and functionality of your structure.

**Steps:**
1. **Introduce Faults:** Introduce intentional faults into your structure, such as loose connections, unsupported sections, or faulty mechanisms. Document the faults and their locations.
2. **Analyze Failures:** Observe how the faults affect the structure's functionality. Take photos or videos of the failures and note any changes in behavior.
3. **Debug Issues:** Identify the root causes of the failures and make necessary adjustments to fix the issues. Test the structure again to ensure the faults have been resolved.
4. **Document Your Findings:** Take photos or videos of the debugging process and the final structure. Label key components and note any changes made.

**Expected Output:**
- Photos or videos of the faults and their effects.
- A list of root causes and changes made.
- Inspection notes on structural integrity and precision after debugging.

**State Inspection Steps:**
- Visually inspect the structure for any wobbly parts or unsupported sections.
- Measure critical dimensions using a ruler or measuring tape to ensure precision.
- Test each mechanism individually to ensure it works as intended.
