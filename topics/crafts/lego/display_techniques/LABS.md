# Hands-On Operational Lab: Display Techniques
## Objective & Lab Prerequisites

### Objective
The objective of this lab is to explore, build, and deploy advanced display techniques using LEGO. This includes understanding the structural integrity of LEGO models, configuring them for optimal display, and troubleshooting common issues.

### Prerequisites
- A set of LEGO bricks (preferably a large set with a variety of pieces)
- A flat, stable surface for building
- A digital camera or smartphone for documentation
- Basic understanding of LEGO building techniques

## Exercise 1: Exploration, Diagnostics & System State Inspection

### Task 1: Exploring LEGO Models
- **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with different LEGO models and their structural components.
- **Actions:**
  1. Choose a LEGO model from your set.
  2. Disassemble the model into its individual pieces.
  3. Sort the pieces by type (e.g., bricks, plates, beams).

### Task 2: Diagnostics and Inspection
- **Objective:** Inspect the quality and condition of your LEGO pieces.
- **Actions:**
  1. Check for any damaged or worn pieces.
  2. Ensure all pieces are clean and free of dirt or debris.
  3. Document any issues found.

### Expected Output
- A sorted list of LEGO pieces.
- A documented list of any damaged or worn pieces.

## Exercise 2: Building, Configuring & Deploying a Production Scenario

### Task 1: Building a Complex LEGO Model
- **Objective:** Build a complex LEGO model using the techniques learned in Exercise 1.
- **Actions:**
  1. Choose a complex LEGO model from your set.
  2. Follow the instructions provided with the model.
  3. Pay attention to structural integrity and balance.

### Task 2: Configuring the Display
- **Objective:** Configure the LEGO model for optimal display.
- **Actions:**
  1. Choose a location for the display that provides adequate lighting and space.
  2. Position the model to showcase its features.
  3. Document the setup process.

### Expected Output
- A fully assembled LEGO model.
- A photograph of the model in its display position.

## Exercise 3: Fault Injection, Failure Analysis & Debugging

### Task 1: Introducing a Fault
- **Objective:** Introduce a fault into the LEGO model to simulate a common issue.
- **Actions:**
  1. Deliberately misalign a critical piece in the model.
  2. Observe the effects of the misalignment on the model's stability.

### Task 2: Diagnosing the Fault
- **Objective:** Diagnose the fault and determine its cause.
- **Actions:**
  1. Inspect the model for any signs of misalignment or instability.
  2. Identify the misaligned piece.
  3. Correct the misalignment and test the model's stability.

### Expected Output
- A documented description of the fault and its effects.
- A corrected model with restored stability.

## Challenge Lab: Independent Advanced Scenarios

### Task 1: Advanced Display Techniques
- **Objective:** Create an advanced display setup using multiple LEGO models.
- **Actions:**
  1. Choose multiple LEGO models from your set.
  2. Plan a display setup that showcases the models in an aesthetically pleasing manner.
  3. Build and configure the display setup.
  4. Document the setup process and final display.

### Task 2: Troubleshooting Complex Issues
- **Objective:** Troubleshoot a complex issue in a multi-model display.
- **Actions:**
  1. Introduce a complex fault into the multi-model display (e.g., a piece that supports multiple models is misaligned).
  2. Diagnose and correct the fault.
  3. Document the troubleshooting process and the corrected display.

### Expected Output
- A photograph of the advanced display setup.
- A documented description of the complex fault and the troubleshooting process.

---

This lab guide provides a comprehensive approach to mastering LEGO display techniques, from basic building to advanced troubleshooting. Enjoy your LEGO building journey!
